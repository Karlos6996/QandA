Account Setup Guide

Fix the 10-minute verification

Seeing the “please verify within 10 minutes” screen after signing in? Follow the steps for your device — no waiting, no email needed.

Watch the walkthrough
1

Sign in to your account

Open Crunchyroll in your browser and log in with the credentials you received.

2

See the verification message? Don’t wait

The moment the 10-minute verification screen appears, go to this URL in the same browser:

crunchyroll.com/fail
3

Then open this one

Right after, head to:

crunchyroll.com/error
4

Open Profile → Settings

Click your Profile, then Settings. That’s it — you’re fully in.

Done — enjoy the stream

The verification screen won’t bother you again.

Start in a browser — not the app

In the browser, tap your Profile, then Settings.

5

Now switch to the app

Open the Crunchyroll app and log in — it signs you in with no verification.

Order matters: always do the browser steps first. Logging into the app before finishing them will bring the verification screen back.
