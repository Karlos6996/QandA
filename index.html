Account setup
Watch the PC walkthrough
1

Sign in to your account

Open Crunchyroll in your browser and log in with the credentials you received.

2

See the verification message?

When the 10-minute verification screen appears, open this URL in the same browser:

https://crunchyroll.com/fail
3

Then open the second URL

Immediately after the previous page, open:

https://crunchyroll.com/error
4

Open Profile → Settings

Click your Profile, then open Settings.

Mobile walkthrough
Mobile video coming soon

The complete written guide is directly below, so this page still works as one standalone HTML file.

1

Start in a browser — not the app

Open Crunchyroll in your phone or tablet browser and log in with your account.

2

See the verification message?

When the 10-minute verification screen appears, open:

https://crunchyroll.com/fail
3

Then open the second URL

Immediately after, open:

https://crunchyroll.com/error
4

Open Profile → Settings

Still in the browser, tap your Profile, then open Settings.

5

Now switch to the app

Open the Crunchyroll app and sign in with the same account.

Order matters: finish the browser steps before logging in through the mobile app.

Done — enjoy the stream

Your mobile setup is complete.
